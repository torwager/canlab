Papers you have starred

Star any paper (☆) on the publications page or on its own page to keep it here. Save the current selection under a name to build several reading lists (a course syllabus, a grant, a journal club). Export sends a list to your reference manager: BibTeX or RIS for Zotero, Mendeley, EndNote and Paperpile, a DOI list to paste into an identifier importer, a CSV, or formatted citations.

Without an account the lists are stored in this browser only: use Export to save one or move it to another device, and Import to load it back.
